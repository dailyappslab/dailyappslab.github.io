Fast-food protein and calories, US menus

259 menu items at McDonald's, Burger King, Subway, Starbucks and Chipotle, with calories and protein for every row and carbs, fat, sodium, sugars or caffeine where the chain publishes them. Every number was copied from the chain's own US nutrition page, and each row says the date it was read (17 to 22 October 2026).
